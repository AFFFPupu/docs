## Submit Your Project

Have you created something amazing with EduArc? We'd love to feature your work!

<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare Your Submission">
  Gather information about your project including:
  - Project name and description
  - Screenshots or demo video
  - Link to live site
  - What makes your project unique
</Step>

<Step title="Share Your Story">
  Tell us about your experience building with EduArc
  
  <Tip>
  Share any challenges you overcame or innovative features you implemented
  </Tip>
</Step>

<Step title="Submit">
  Send your submission to our community team for review
  
  <Card title="Get in Touch" icon="envelope" href="mailto:community@eduarc.com">
    Email us your project details
  </Card>
</Step>
</Steps>
