EduArc AI provides a comprehensive suite of tools to help educators create, manage, and publish educational content efficiently. Here are the core features that power your teaching workflow.

## Upload PDF Content

Transform your existing teaching materials into interactive digital content with just a few clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Your PDF">
    Upload any PDF file containing your teaching notes, lecture materials, or course content.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="AI Processing">
    Our AI automatically analyzes and structures your content, extracting key information and organizing it into a clean format.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review & Refine">
    Review the extracted content and make any necessary adjustments before publishing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Supported formats:** PDF files up to 50MB. The AI works best with text-based PDFs rather than scanned images.
</Note>

### What Gets Extracted

When you upload a PDF, EduArc AI intelligently identifies and extracts:

- **Headings and structure** - Maintains your document hierarchy
- **Key concepts** - Identifies important definitions and terminology
- **Examples and explanations** - Preserves context and teaching points
- **Visual elements** - Captures important diagrams and figures (where applicable)

## Edit with AI Agent

Collaborate with an intelligent AI agent to refine and perfect your educational content.

### Interactive Editing

The AI agent acts as your teaching assistant, helping you improve content through natural conversation:

```
You: "Make the introduction more engaging for high school students"
AI: "I'll add a relatable hook and adjust the tone..."

You: "Add a real-world example for photosynthesis"
AI: "Here's an example using a garden scenario..."
```

### Smart Suggestions

The AI agent proactively offers improvements:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Clarity" icon="glasses">
    Suggests clearer wording for complex concepts
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Structure" icon="sitemap">
    Recommends better organization and flow
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Engagement" icon="heart">
    Adds interactive elements and examples
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Assessment" icon="clipboard-check">
    Generates questions to test understanding
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Iterative Refinement

Work iteratively with the AI to:

- Add, remove, or reorganize sections
- Adjust difficulty and pacing
- Insert multimedia placeholders
- Create custom learning components
- Align with curriculum standards

<Warning>
  Always review AI-generated content for accuracy and appropriateness for your specific teaching context.
</Warning>

## Publish Your Content

Share your polished educational materials with students in just one click.

### Publishing Options

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web Link">
    Generate a shareable link that students can access from any device.
    
    **Perfect for:**
    - Online and hybrid classes
    - Remote learning
    - Student self-study
    - Parent access to materials
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Embed Code">
    Get an embed code to integrate content directly into your:
    
    - Learning Management System (LMS)
    - School website
    - Course portal
    - Educational blog
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Export">
    Download your content in various formats:
    
    - PDF for printing
    - HTML for offline access
    - SCORM package for LMS integration
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### What Students See

Once published, students get access to:

- Clean, mobile-responsive content
- Interactive components (quizzes, flashcards, definitions)
- Progress tracking and bookmarks
- Search functionality
- Night mode for comfortable reading

### Version Control

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft Mode">
    Work on content privately before publishing
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Publish">
    Make content live for students
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Update Anytime">
    Edit and republish - students always see the latest version
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Published content remains accessible via the same link even after updates, so you never need to redistribute links to students.
</Info>
