## How to Generate Content

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose Content Type">
  Select the type of content you want to generate from the dashboard.
  
  <Info>
  Different content types have different generation parameters and options.
  </Info>
</Step>

<Step title="Provide Input">
  Enter your prompt, topic, or source material for generation.
  
  ```text
  Example prompt: "Create a beginner-friendly tutorial about 
  Python programming covering variables, loops, and functions"
  ```
  
  <Tip>
  Be specific and detailed in your prompts for better results.
  </Tip>
</Step>

<Step title="Configure Settings">
  Adjust generation parameters like tone, length, and complexity.
  
  <Tabs>
  <Tab title="Tone">
    - Professional
    - Casual
    - Academic
    - Conversational
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Length">
    - Short (500 words)
    - Medium (1000 words)
    - Long (2000+ words)
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Complexity">
    - Beginner
    - Intermediate
    - Advanced
  </Tab>
  </Tabs>
</Step>

<Step title="Generate and Review">
  Click generate and review the output.
  
  <Check>
  Your generated content will appear in the editor where you can review and refine it.
  </Check>
</Step>

<Step title="Edit and Refine">
  Make any necessary adjustments to the generated content.
  
  <Warning>
  Always review AI-generated content for accuracy and appropriateness before publishing.
  </Warning>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Generation Parameters

<ParamField body="prompt" type="string" required>
  The input text or topic that describes what content you want to generate.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="content_type" type="string" required>
  Type of content to generate: `article`, `tutorial`, `quiz`, `video_script`, or `image_description`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="tone" type="string" default="professional">
  The tone of the generated content: `professional`, `casual`, `academic`, or `conversational`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="length" type="integer" default="1000">
  Target length in words (for text) or seconds (for video). Range: 100-5000.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="target_audience" type="string" default="general">
  Target audience level: `beginner`, `intermediate`, `advanced`, or `expert`.
</ParamField>

## Best Practices

<Tip>
- Write clear, detailed prompts for better results
- Specify your target audience and desired tone
- Review and edit generated content before publishing
- Use generation as a starting point, not the final product
- Experiment with different parameters to find what works best
</Tip>

```text Tutorial

React development environment, including Node.js installation, 
creating a new project, and running the first app.
```


```text Article
focusing on solar and wind power, their benefits, challenges, 
and future potential. Target audience: general public.
```
</CodeGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Generation takes too long">
  - Check your internet connection
  - Try reducing the target length
  - Simplify your prompt
  - Try again during off-peak hours
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Output quality is poor">
  - Make your prompt more specific and detailed
  - Adjust tone and complexity settings
  - Provide more context in your input
  - Try regenerating with different parameters
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Generation failed">
  - Verify your prompt meets content guidelines
  - Check that you have available generation credits
  - Ensure your input isn't too long or complex
  - Contact support if the issue persists
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
