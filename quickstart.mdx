## Get started with EduArc AI in four steps

Start creating, enhancing, and sharing educational content with AI in just minutes.

### Step 1: Create an account or sign in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="user-plus" title="Register or Sign In">
    Head to the [EduArc AI platform](https://www.eduarc.ai/) and create your free account or log in if you already have one.<br /><br />
    Access your personal workspace to start managing and generating teaching materials.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 2: Create a New Project

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="folder-plus" title="Start Your Project">
    Click <b>New Project</b> in your dashboard.<br />
    <ul>
      <li>Give your project a name.</li>
    </ul>
    <video controls className="w-full aspect-video" src="/Create Project.mp4">
      Your browser does not support the video tag.
    </video>
    <br />
    <ul>
      <li>Choose to <b>upload your PDF notes</b> or let the AI help generate materials for you.</li>
    </ul>
    <video controls className="w-full aspect-video" src="/Upload pdf.mp4">
      Your browser does not support the video tag.
    </video>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 3: Review and Edit with AI

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="comments" title="Chat & Edit with AI">
    Work together with the AI agent to refine your content and structure.<br />
    <ul>
      <li>Ask questions or request changes using the AI chat.</li>
      <li>Restructure sections, add media, or clarify concepts as you wish.</li>
    </ul>
    <Tip>
      Explore more advanced features in the <b><a href="/features/uploading-content">Features</a></b> tab.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
