## Get started with EduArc AI in four steps

Start creating, enhancing, and sharing educational content with AI in just minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account or sign in" icon="user-plus">
    Head to the [EduArc AI platform](https://www.eduarc.ai/) and create your free account or log in if you already have one.

    Access your personal workspace to start managing and generating teaching materials.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a New Project" icon="folder-plus">
    Click **New Project** in your dashboard.

    - Give your project a name.

    <video controls className="w-full aspect-video" src="/Create Project.mp4">
      Your browser does not support the video tag.
    </video>

    - Choose to **upload your PDF notes** or let the AI help generate materials for you.

    <video controls className="w-full aspect-video" src="/Upload pdf.mp4">
      Your browser does not support the video tag.
    </video>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and Edit with AI" icon="comments">
    Work together with the AI agent to refine your content and structure.

    - Ask questions or request changes using the AI chat.
    - Restructure sections, add media, or clarify concepts as you wish.

    <Tip>
      Explore more advanced features in the **[Features](/features/uploading-content)** tab.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and Share" icon="rocket">
    When you're satisfied with your content, hit **Publish** to make your educational materials live.

    Share your project link with students or colleagues!
  </Step>
</Steps>
